Investment Stories

Eight investments — breakout exits, active bets, and an honest write-off. Each shaped how I think about founders, markets, and risk.

Exponent Energy

Investment Lead Active

EV / Energy · Bengaluru · Fund II & III

Thesis

India's 3W cargo segment was overlooked by investors chasing passenger EVs. Exponent built refuelling-speed charging — a genuinely different product — backed by deep hardware IP and OEM partnerships.

My Role

Investment Lead & Board Observer. Led Series B syndication, monitored MIS and burn against AOP, guided CFO hire.

Outcome

8,000 e^pack orders locked. Series B at INR 6,000 Mn pre-money from Eight Roads (fully subscribed). 32 patents filed.

Key Learning

Hardware moats are real. Patent density + OEM lock-in creates defensibility pure software rarely achieves at the early stage.

Wiom

Investment Lead Active

Consumer / Telecom · Gurugram · Fund III

Thesis

PM-WANI gave Wiom a license telcos structurally can't hold. We backed the regulatory asymmetry before the market consensus caught up — betting that last-mile public Wi-Fi would follow the same buildout as mobile towers.

My Role

Investment Lead & Board Observer. Drove oversubscribed Series A syndication, flagged CFO gap early, tracked monthly ARR and city-by-city expansion milestones.

Outcome

12× revenue in 15 months. US$2.5M ARR. Series A oversubscribed. Expanding Delhi → Mumbai → tier-2 cities.

Key Learning

Regulatory moats can outlast IP moats. The job is finding structural asymmetry before the consensus does — and having the conviction to back it early.

QpiAI

Investment Lead Active

Quantum / AI · Bengaluru · Fund II

Thesis

One of the few teams globally building both the platform and application layer for quantum-AI. An inevitable market, a rare team — the bet was on the team's technology depth, with patience required for market timing.

My Role

Investment Lead & Board Observer. Developed internal investment thesis, monitored R&D and pilot milestones, shaped fundraising narrative towards institutional investors.

Status

Active. Scaling pilots in industrial and defence. One of India's most credible quantum-AI platforms.

Key Learning

Deep tech: separate technology risk from market timing risk. QpiAI had low tech risk (world-class team, strong IP) — the thesis accepted high timing risk consciously.

Uniphore

Analyst Exited · 7.7× MOIC

Enterprise AI · Chennai · Fund I

Thesis

Conversational AI for enterprise call centres before "AI" was a category. After 3 pivots, real PMF with large BPOs who couldn't function without it. John Chambers (ex-Cisco CEO) backing gave rare global credibility.

My Role

Analyst. Supported valuation analysis across funding rounds using DCF and market approaches. Contributed to exit structuring and secondary transaction logistics.

Outcome

Exited at 7.7× MOIC. Scaled to SE Asia and USA. Global enterprise conversation-intelligence platform.

Key Learning

Founders made future access conditional on joining a 2020 secondary. We participated. Liquidity windows in VC are finite — missing them costs more than waiting for a bigger multiple.

Arya.ai

Investment Lead Exited

FinTech / AI · Mumbai · Fund I

Thesis

Enterprise AI for BFSI with real institutional customers and a path to 40% EBITDA margins — genuinely rare in India at the time. Explainable AI for compliance-heavy banks was a strong moat.

My Role

Investment Lead & Board Observer. Navigated multiple failed term sheets without losing conviction, flagged a founder equity dispute early and pushed for resolution, guided exit banker appointment.

Outcome

Exited. 9× ARR growth YoY. 40% EBITDA margin. PAT positive. 2 patents. Clean exit above entry valuation.

Key Learning

Governance issues don't self-resolve. Catching the founder equity dispute early and pushing for resolution enabled a clean exit — delayed action would have killed it.

SmartQ

Analyst Acquired · Compass Group

FoodTech · Bengaluru · Fund I

Thesis

Saving 15–45 min per corporate employee per meal — a "need to have" with exceptional retention across marquee accounts like Compass Group. First-mover in a market with low replication risk.

My Role

Analyst. Supported YourNest-assisted bridge rounds to maintain runway, tracked monthly MIS and order volume, contributed to exit transaction management.

Outcome

Acquired by Compass Group — a global food services giant and their own marquee customer. Daily orders: 150 → 150,000.

Key Learning

The best acquirer is often already your customer. Customer delight is an underrated M&A signal — Compass Group bought what they couldn't afford to lose.

Argoid

Investment Lead Exited

AI / E-Commerce · Singapore · Fund II

Thesis

ML personalisation for e-commerce with distribution potential across SEA and the US. A rare emerging-market AI play with genuine global reach from a Singapore base.

My Role

Investment Lead. Led cross-border secondary exit after a downround — managed LP communications, legal structuring, and Singapore entity complexities.

Outcome

Exited via cross-border secondary. Pre-money fell US$12M → US$9M but capital was returned to LPs.

Key Learning

A managed exit beats a write-off. Executing cleanly under difficult conditions — downround, cross-border, unhappy LPs — is a distinct and learnable skill.

Koinearth

Investment Lead Write-off

Blockchain · Bengaluru · Fund II

Thesis

Blockchain for B2B supply chain at a moment when enterprise blockchain was gaining traction. Real problem (inventory visibility + invoice financing), credible team, addressable market.

My Role

Investment Lead. Monitored pivot decisions, raised governance flags as strategy shifted, managed the acquisition process to minimise LP losses.

Outcome

Written off. Pivoted from supply chain to NFTs, failed to scale, acquired at negligible value.

Key Learning

Thesis drift toward a hype cycle signals desperation, not conviction. We now pressure-test every pivot against this question explicitly at board level.
